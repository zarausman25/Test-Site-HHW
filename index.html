Commercial waste goes elsewhere

Businesses, schools, churches, non-profits, landlords and contractors

Household hazardous waste facilities are limited by Texas regulation to waste generated by households. That rule covers organizations of every size, including small non-profits and places of worship, and it applies to the Harris County facility, the City of Houston service centers, and the other programs listed on this page.

Where to go instead: contact the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality and ask for the GI-225 Vendor List for Region 12 (Houston/Galveston). It lists permitted contractors who handle commercial hazardous waste.
